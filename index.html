Privacy Policy

Pervasive Autonomy Lab VR applications
Effective October 2, 2026

Who we are and what this policy covers

The Pervasive Autonomy Lab at the University of California, Irvine ("PAL VR," "we," or "us") operates experimental VR applications, including sorting applications, for supervised, noncommercial research. This policy covers data processed through those applications on Meta Quest. Each research study may also provide participants with a study information sheet explaining its specific procedures.

Information we process

  • Lab Meta account data. Participants use lab-controlled Meta accounts during sessions. The applications use Meta Horizon User ID and User Profile platform features to run multiplayer sessions. These account fields are used during sessions and are not retained by PAL VR as research records.
  • Multiplayer and session data. The applications process connection and session information, including data needed to join a shared session and synchronize interactions. PAL VR retains session logs for research. Photon, operated by Exit Games, relays multiplayer traffic and may process technical data such as a network user identifier and IP address to provide its service.
  • Recorded study data. Depending on the study, the research team may collect headset and controller sensor data, head and hand movement, eye gaze, interactions with virtual objects, audio and video recordings, and survey responses. The Group Collaboration in AR/VR/MR study information sheet (UCI IRB #5774) describes data collected for that study.
  • Communications. If you contact the research team, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message.

How we use information

We use Meta account, connection, and real-time interaction data to connect lab devices and operate shared VR activities. We use session logs and recorded study data to analyze collaboration and conduct the research described in the applicable study information sheet. We use communications to answer questions and handle data requests.

Who receives information

Other participants in a shared session can perceive actions and content needed for the group activity. Meta provides account and platform services. Photon processes technical connection data and relays multiplayer traffic. For UCI IRB #5774, the research team, authorized UCI personnel, and regulatory entities may access study records as described in the information sheet. De-identified research information may be shared with other researchers for future studies as described there.

Storage and security

For UCI IRB #5774, identifiable research information is replaced with a code, and the list linking codes to identities is kept separately. De-identified research data is stored on a secure UCI enterprise cloud platform. Identifiable audio and video recordings are stored in an encrypted, password-protected file on a laptop computer, as stated in the study information sheet. PAL VR retains session logs as research data. Photon and Meta handle information under their own services and policies.

Retention

For UCI IRB #5774, research information, including retained session logs, is kept for ten years after the end of the calendar year in which the research is completed, as described in the study information sheet. Identifiable audio and video recordings are erased at the end of that study. Other studies may have different retention schedules stated in their information sheets. PAL VR uses lab Meta account fields during sessions and does not retain those fields as research records. Meta and Photon may retain technical data under their own policies.

Your choices and deletion requests

Study participation is voluntary. The applicable information sheet explains how to withdraw from a study. To request access to or deletion of data associated with you, email dgromer1@uci.edu and identify the application and study. The research team will explain what data it can locate or delete and whether a research retention requirement limits deletion. Requests concerning data controlled by Meta or Photon may need to be directed to those providers.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our application data practices change.

Contact

For privacy or application questions, contact Diana Romero at dgromer1@uci.edu. For questions about rights as a participant in UCI IRB #5774, contact the UCI Institutional Review Board at IRB@research.uci.edu.
