Who we are and what this policy covers

The Pervasive Autonomy Lab at the University of California, Irvine ("PAL VR," "we," or "us") operates experimental VR applications, including sorting applications, for supervised, noncommercial research. This policy applies to those applications when used on Meta Quest and describes the data we process through them. A study may give participants a separate information sheet with additional research details.
